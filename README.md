# Eye-Disease-Classification
Eye diseases such as cataract, diabetic retinopathy, and glaucoma are among the leading causes of preventable blindness globally. Early detection is crucial for effective treatment. However, manual screening requires expert ophthalmologists, a process that is time-consuming, costly, and not scalable, especially in resource-limited regions.
